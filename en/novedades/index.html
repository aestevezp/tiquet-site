What's new

What each version of Tiquet brings, newest first. How each part works is in the guide.

Version 2.1

  • A new Home, clearer and with less scrolling: at the top, today, the month's ticket (tap a group for its report) and «This week», with what is charged each day and what you have to do; scrolling down, the month, in cards with their figure and a small chart, and «More of your home». In the guide →
  • «All of Tiquet»: the grid button at the top shows every part of your home with what it says today, including what you've hidden from Home. In the guide →
  • To hide the amounts, hold the total of the month's ticket; hold it again to show them. In the guide →
  • «Ask Tiquet», the whole width, with example questions. In the guide →
  • Budget and Pace explain what you see, and the budget changes from its page. Savings goals, in «Settings» too. In the guide →
  • «Where to spend less», clearer. In the guide →
  • Home insurance: the building sum insured, and long policies read better. In the guide →
  • The basket shows all your products, with search. In the guide →
  • What Tiquet tells you on Home is kept in «Settings» › «Messages from Tiquet»; your accounts, in «Settings» › «From your bank» too. In the guide →
  • Better with large text: figures and buttons no longer break. Thousands always marked.
  • Fixes: bar visits repeated when two devices synced, cards that closed by themselves, and Horizon from its button.
  • Prefer the Home you had? Switch off «Home on tickets» in «Settings» › «Look». In the guide →

  • Tiquet, now also in Catalan. Paste a Google Maps link and it becomes the address. Faster with years of statements. In the guide →
  • Apple Pay: the receipt of a payment with a tip is no longer counted twice. In the guide →
